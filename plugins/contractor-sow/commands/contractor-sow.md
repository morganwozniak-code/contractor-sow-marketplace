description: Start the contractor intake form and automatically generate a draft SOW or agreement package when required fields are complete
---

Run the `contractor-sow-drafter` skill. Classify the request as net-new, renewal,
scope change, fee change, or replacement SOW, then present the structured form from
`skills/contractor-sow-drafter/intake-form.md`, merge the requester's answers across
turns, show `NEEDS INFORMATION`, `BLOCKED`, or `READY TO DRAFT`, and automatically
generate the full agreement package, SOW-only update, or neutral draft SOW when the
form is complete. Preserve all of the skill's engagement-type, legal-review,
source-template, and external-system safeguards.
